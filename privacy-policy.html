1. About this policy

This policy explains how Be Python, an iOS Snake game, handles personal information through account registration, sign-in, password reset, profile management and the leaderboard. It covers the current Spark-only implementation. Some features shown in the app are not yet available, as explained below. Reading and acknowledging this policy is not a waiver of your privacy rights or blanket consent to unrelated processing.

2. Information we collect

Account information. Firebase Authentication processes your email address, Firebase user ID, sign-in provider information and authentication credentials. For email/password registration, your password is sent to Firebase Authentication to establish and authenticate your account. The app does not store plaintext passwords in Firestore, source code or its diagnostic logs. Google Sign-In supplies identity information and authentication credentials to Google and Firebase; your authentication profile may include your Google display name and profile image.

Profile information. Cloud Firestore stores your Firebase user ID, chosen username and normalized username, email address, profile image reference fields, level, high score, total score, creation/update timestamps, and the accepted Privacy Policy version and acceptance timestamp. Newly created profiles start at level 1 and scores of 0. The last-played field starts empty and is not currently updated by gameplay. A separate username reservation links the normalized username to your user ID.

Game and device-local information. The app stores an account-specific high score, sound setting, speed setting and Remember Me preference on your device. Game state is used during play. Current gameplay results are not uploaded to Firestore or submitted for online ranking. Existing cloud score fields may still be displayed. Signing out does not erase your local score records or preferences.

Photos. You may select a photo through the iOS photo picker for an on-device preview. The selected image is resized/compressed in memory. Cloud photo upload is unavailable in the current app, and selecting a photo does not upload it. Existing profile image references, if present, may be loaded from their hosting service, which receives the image request.

Technical information. Requests to Firebase and Google necessarily involve network and service information such as IP addresses, service request details and authentication/device-integrity information. Firebase App Check uses Apple's App Attest or DeviceCheck in release builds to help verify legitimate app requests. Development builds use a private debug provider. These services process information according to their own policies. The app's own DEBUG diagnostics restrict output to operation stages and selected error codes; they do not intentionally log passwords, tokens or email addresses.

The reviewed app does not integrate advertising, Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics, or request location, contacts or tracking permission. This statement describes this implementation and does not mean authentication services collect no technical information.

3. How information is used

We use information to create and authenticate accounts, restore sessions according to your preference, send requested password-reset messages, maintain your profile, reserve unique usernames, display leaderboard entries, remember settings and local scores, protect accounts and services, diagnose failures and respond to privacy requests. Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing may rely on providing requested account services, legitimate interests in security and maintenance, legal obligations, or consent where legally required. The developer must identify the applicable jurisdiction and any required specific legal basis before release.

5. Leaderboard visibility

Leaderboard profiles contain user ID, username, profile image reference, level and high score. The current app shows the top 100 profiles ordered primarily by cloud high score with deterministic tie-breakers. The prepared Firestore rules allow signed-in users to read these profiles; they are not private to you. Do not use a username or photo that reveals information you do not want other players to see. Private email and other private profile fields are not included in leaderboard documents.

The current app does not synchronize game results to cloud ranking. Initial cloud scores remain 0 unless separately maintained through an authorized administrative process. Local high scores are not represented as server-verified scores. Rules publication and actual backend permissions must be checked before release.

6. Storage and retention

Authentication and cloud profiles are held by Firebase. Local settings and account-specific score records are held on your device; authentication sessions are managed by the Firebase SDK. The Firestore client is configured to use an in-memory cache. Cloud service infrastructure may process data outside your country. The developer must confirm the deployed database location and applicable international-transfer safeguards before publication; a fixed storage country has not been verified.

Accounts and profiles are retained while the account exists unless the developer removes them following a verified request or applicable legal requirement. No automatic inactivity-deletion period is implemented. Signing out does not delete cloud records. Removing the app does not delete your Firebase account; locally stored data may remain subject to iOS backup and restore behavior. Necessary service records or backups may remain for provider retention periods or legal/security requirements after deletion. We have not verified a fixed purge deadline and do not promise immediate removal from all backups.

7. Account deletion and privacy requests

Automated in-app account deletion is not currently implemented. The Delete Account screen reports that cleanup is unavailable and does not delete the account or claim success. To request deletion, contact Babanzana198@gmail.com from your account email where possible. Do not send your password, authentication tokens or government identification unless a justified verification method is separately explained.

The developer must verify ownership and manually coordinate removal of the Firebase Authentication account, private and public Firestore profiles, username reservation and any associated personal records or hosted image references that actually exist. Requests concerning local data require separate device steps; the current app has no remote local-data erasure feature. The developer must establish a working deletion process before App Store release and confirm completion accurately.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, obtain a copy of, delete or restrict use of your information, object to certain processing, withdraw consent where consent is the legal basis, or complain to a competent privacy authority. Use the contact above to request assistance. We may need proportionate ownership verification. Availability, exceptions and response periods depend on applicable law; withdrawing consent does not invalidate lawful earlier processing.

8. Children's privacy

Minimum account age and intended audience: Be Python is intended for users aged 13 and older. Users under 13 must not create an account. This is an age requirement stated in this policy; the app does not currently technically verify users' ages or implement age-screening or parental-consent mechanisms.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has registered, please contact Babanzana198@gmail.com so the developer can review the account and coordinate appropriate action, including removal of the child's account and associated personal information where applicable. Do not send the child's password or authentication tokens. Automated in-app account deletion is not currently implemented; the manual cleanup limitations in Section 7 also apply to these requests.

In the United States, collection from children under 13 may require COPPA compliance, including verifiable parental consent when applicable. Other countries may impose different age requirements. A child's selection of the Privacy Policy checkbox is not verifiable parental consent. Stating a minimum age does not itself establish legal compliance; applicable obligations and safeguards must be assessed before release.

9. Security

We use Firebase Authentication, encrypted service connections and ownership-based Firestore rules designed to separate private profiles from leaderboard data and protect username reservations. The developer must verify the deployed rules and App Check configuration. These measures reduce risk but do not guarantee absolute security. Use a strong, unique password and keep your account access private. The app never asks you to send passwords or tokens in a privacy request.
